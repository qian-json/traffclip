Help

  1. Watch. Click any camera. Video runs about 20 seconds behind.
  2. Clip. Press the clapperboard, or its key, to keep the last 30 seconds.
  3. Record. The red dot starts a recording. The square ends it.
  4. Save. Open Clips to download your videos.
  5. Go big. Expand fills the page. The window icon floats a camera while you browse.
  6. Find. Search, pick a region, star favorites, or switch to the map.
  7. Scan. Lists cameras turned away from their usual view, often toward a crash or work zone.
