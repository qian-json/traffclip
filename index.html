  1. Watch. Hover a camera and click the play icon in its corner to start the live feed. Click the stop icon to end it. You see the road about 20 seconds late.
  2. Go big. Click the corner icon to open a camera large. Press Esc to return to the grid.
  3. Pop out. Click the window icon to watch a camera in a small window. Drag its bar to move it and its corner to resize it.
  4. Clip. Press clip to grab the last 30 seconds you watched. Change that length in settings.
  5. Keys. Bind a key with the key button. Press that key to clip without the mouse.
  6. Record. Press rec to start and stop to finish. The red timer shows how long you've recorded.
  7. Save. You'll find your clips in the list at the top right. Save the whole list or single clips.
  8. Merge. Turn on merge overlaps in settings to join overlapping clips from one camera into one video.
  9. Favorites. Star a camera to mark it. Press the star in the header to see only your favorites.
  10. Map. Switch to map to find cameras by location. Drag to move, scroll to zoom, and click a dot to open its camera.
  11. Find. Type a road or pick a region to narrow the grid and the map.
