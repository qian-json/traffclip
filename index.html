traffclip
  1. Watch. Click a camera to start its live feed. Click it a second time to stop. You see the road about 20 seconds late.
  2. Go big. Click the corner icon to open a camera large. Press Esc to return to the grid.
  3. Clip. Press clip to grab the last 30 seconds you watched. Change that length in settings.
  4. Keys. Bind a key with the key button. Press that key to clip without the mouse.
  5. Record. Press rec to start and stop to finish. The red timer shows how long you've recorded.
  6. Save. You'll find your clips in the list at the top right. Save the whole list or single clips.
  7. Merge. Tick merge overlaps to join overlapping clips from one camera into one video.
  8. Find. Type a road or pick a region to narrow the grid.
